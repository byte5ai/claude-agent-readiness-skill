# Qualitative assessment rubric

These aspects cannot be checked binarily and require semantic judgment of the content. Apply to 2–3 sampled subpages (landing + 1 content page + 1 API/docs page, depending on site type).

**Scale 1–5.** Each aspect gets a number and 1–2 sentences of justification with a concrete piece of evidence from the page (quote or paragraph reference). **Where possible, cite or reference — do not work from gut feel.**

| Aspect | What is assessed | 1 (weak) | 3 (average) | 5 (strong) | Tier · Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fact density** | Verifiable claims per paragraph. Concrete numbers, dates, sources. | Prose without hard facts, adjectives dominate | Some verifiable statements, mostly assertion | Every paragraph contains at least one checkable fact with evidence | A 🟢 (Princeton GEO — Statistics top lever) |
| **Answer-first structure** | H2 poses a question, the first paragraph answers it directly. Reasoning follows after. | Layout follows the classic intro → long buildup → late answer | Answer appears within the first 200 words | H2 question + first sentence answers it directly | B 🟡 (Hitches, ZipTie) |
| **Statistics density** | Concrete numbers with date and source attribution. | No numbers or unsupported "up to X%" phrases | Numbers present, partially with source | Every statistic carries a date + verifiable source (study, dataset) | A 🟢 (Princeton) |
| **Expert quote frequency & attribution** | Original quotes from named experts, correctly attributed. | No quotes, or unattributed assertions | Occasional quotes, partially with source | Multiple attributed quotes with affiliation and link where applicable | A 🟢 (Princeton — Quotation Addition top lever) |
| **Linguistic clarity** | Short sentences, clear terminology, few buzzwords, technical terms defined. | Nested sentences, marketing buzzwords, terms unexplained | Understandable with some clutter | Clear, precise, technical language defined or linked | A 🟢 (Princeton — Easy-to-Understand) |
| **E-E-A-T signals** | Author bio with verifiable credentials, organizational context, About page, update date. | No author info, generic "team" signature | Author name + photo, sparse bio | Author with credentials, link to Author schema, linked profile page, visible update date | S 🟢 (Bing AI Performance recommendation); B 🟡 correlation in secondary studies |
| **Structured answer blocks (50–150 words)** | Content breaks into extractable answer chunks (50–150 words per logical unit). | Endless prose paragraphs without clear separation | Some sections chunked, others not | Consistently chunked, every section independently citable | B 🟡 (RAG chunking mechanics plausible, not empirically quantified) |
| **Cross-format consistency** | Same entities/concepts consistent across text, image alts, video transcripts. | Images/videos without alt text or transcripts, isolated text | Some alt text, occasional transcripts | Alt text describes content with entity names, videos have transcripts | S 🟢 (Bing AI Performance) |
| **YouTube/video representation** | Are there video resources that extend the topic? (Only check on sites with marketing relevance.) | None | Sporadic, not embedded | Consistent video strategy, dedicated YouTube presence, embeds on relevant pages | B 🟢 (Ahrefs March 2026 — 18.2% of off-rank AIO citations are YouTube) |

## Aggregate score

Average of the aspect scores. Scale:
- 4.5–5.0 — Strong; content itself is a citation magnet
- 3.5–4.4 — Solid; pull higher with targeted improvements
- 2.5–3.4 — Average; substantial content work required
- 1.0–2.4 — Weak; content is not the problem, this is marketing copy

## Application rules

- **Cite at least 1 concrete example per aspect** from the scanned page. "Vibes" are not an assessment.
- **For API/reference pages**: fact density and statistics density drop naturally — adapt the rubric or skip the aspect.
- **For e-commerce pages**: expert quote frequency is usually not applicable — replace with review substance / spec density.
- **For multilingual sites**: take the sample in **the language** that represents the main market, not automatically English.
- **n=3 is a sample, not a full-text audit**. State explicitly in the report that the aggregate score is based on 3 pages.
